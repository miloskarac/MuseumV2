Implementation and independent review: 2026-11-12. **Complete under the approved development-save policy.** The strict purchase order applies immediately to fresh and loaded games. Historical data is preserved, but obsolete development saves are not guaranteed continued playability; affected players have a safe, explicit manual recovery path. All changes remain uncommitted.

## Active progression and prices

| --- | --- | --- |
| Dimetrodon stand | Initial Room 1 ownership | 12 coins, leaving 8 from the starting 20 |
| Dimetrodon display | Recovered parts assembled on its owned stand | Admission 4 |
| Trilobite stand | Valid Dimetrodon display | 8 coins |

| `eryops-dig` purchase | Owned Eryops stand and the preceding display prefix | 20 coins, separate explicit transaction |

The free initial site remains accessible without any stand. A purchased incomplete stand holds the progression step; it does not unlock another purchase. The second site no longer additionally requires first-site closure. Already owned historical sites remain accessible. No west-wing purchase or credit consumption is involved.

`mvp-progression.ts` adapts the existing three active recipes and owned stands to the Phase 7.6A query API and Phase 7.4B display predicate. It derives fixed stand placement from the existing owned-stand/placed-part model; no parallel ownership store or arbitrary completion list is introduced. Missing recovered finds, duplicate placed parts, ambiguous stand receipts and invalid room ownership fail closed. Existing graph tests retain invalid placement and duplicate exhibit-record coverage.

Only the three active fossils supply recipes to the adapter. Walchia, future sites, Room 2 and Room 3 remain inactive. Three displayed fossils complete the MVP sequence, **not Room 1**: its four-exhibit completion rule is unchanged. Room 1 geometry, stand coordinates, camera, collision dimensions, excavation and cleaning rules are unchanged.

## Admission and reachability

Valid large exhibits contribute 4 and small exhibits 2; no valid display means no paying visitors. Visitor arrival rates, queue/occupancy caps, two-stop tours and manual cash collection are unchanged. Existing soil-coin outcomes are also unchanged; “no first-room income” is applied to museum admission, without removing the separately existing digging rewards.

The deterministic ticket-only witness uses a synthetic recovered first site whose soil rewards are excluded, so its proof does not depend on digging luck: 20 → buy Dimetrodon for 12 → 8; display Dimetrodon → admission 4; buy Trilobite for 8 → 0; display Trilobite → admission 6; six collected ticket sales → 36; buy Eryops stand for 16 → 20; buy the site for 20 → 0. Eryops can then be collected and assembled. Optional upgrades/helpers cannot spend money before the first paying display, and spending afterward cannot remove recurring ticket income.

Unpaid visitors use the price at service time. Paid visitors retain the price already recorded. The existing extensible ledger stores sales by actual price; pending cash remains separate until collected. A 4-coin ticket followed by a 6-coin ticket remains 10 coins of banknotes. The admission increase cue now shows the actual delta, including +4/+2/+4, rather than its previous hard-coded +1.

## Schema 25 and historical evidence

Changing Eryops stand/site prices and purchase order requires an explicit next schema. Schema 25 adds only `purchaseHistory`:

```text
version: 1
standIds: inherited museum purchase IDs (including any historical wing receipt)
siteIds: inherited paid/legacy site IDs
deposits: original partial-payment amounts
sales: original price-keyed ticket ledger
```

These fields record migration evidence, not new ownership or spendable balances. Ownership continues to reside in the existing museum/world receipt arrays. Migration first validates through the schema-24 reader, then preserves all existing fields and copies the bounded evidence. Historical receipt objects and amounts are not rewritten: an Eryops stand paid at 12 stays paid at 12; a site paid at 24 stays paid at 24. New purchases use 16 and 20. Legacy free receipts retain their source and zero amount. Old wing receipts, deposits and credit remain unchanged and cannot be spent.

Historical Eryops partial payments remain deducted and recorded, now counting toward the 16-coin total. For example, 7 previously paid means 9 still owed. An inherited later-stand deposit is preserved while its new prerequisite is unmet, and cannot grow while locked. It cannot silently disappear or shrink. Wing deposits cannot grow or complete through schema-25 gameplay.

The reader reuses existing financial, journey, helper, custody, room and receipt validation through explicit read rules. Earlier readers retain their default historical prices and semantics; no file under `src/persistence/contracts/` was changed for this phase. New receipts require the preceding displayed prefix. Already-paid historical receipts remain valid evidence of past ownership, with their historical amounts; they do not bypass current purchase prerequisites or permit locked deposits to grow.

Original ticket sales remain at their original prices. New sales must use a reachable sum of current exhibit contributions. Without inherited out-of-order stands, only 4/6/10 are possible. Old non-prefix displays can legitimately produce other combinations such as a 2-coin trilobite display; these are validated against the inherited stand evidence. Financially balanced but impossible current tiers are rejected. The ledger reconciles bank balance + pending cash + actual spending against starting funds, earned soil claims and actual historical/current ticket income.

Local save validation checks consistency, not authenticity against a hostile editor. A fully rewritten snapshot resembling a legitimate old save cannot be cryptographically distinguished without an external trust source; no such service is introduced.

All mutations retain the existing candidate-state commit and retry architecture. `advanceMuseum` preserves schema 25 and its history, and `economyOf` removes history from the older accounting projection. Loader-only paid-visitor retirement and helper recovery retain their established semantics; migration itself does not rewrite paid prices or lose their cash.

## UI

Only the eligible stand has a gold purchase marker/button. Existing floor inlays remain visible and inactive. Completing a display changes the layout's presentation key so the next purchase marker appears immediately without moving any stand. Short Serbian Cyrillic hints explain which fossil unlocks the next stand. The map shows a disabled site purchase with “Прво купи постоље за ериопса.” until eligible, then displays the 20-coin price. The map refresh key includes stand ownership.

The final hint explicitly says three fossils are displayed and Walchia is coming later. It does not claim full Room 1 completion. Existing native buttons, keyboard interactions and touch purchase controls are retained.

On loading a save without a Dimetrodon stand or paying display, insufficient bank balance + pending cash + Dimetrodon deposit triggers a conservative notice explaining the new sequence. Remaining soil may still supply money, so the notice offers **Задржи игру** (keep the save) as well as **Нови почетак** (new beginning). Reset opens a separate destructive confirmation; cancel returns to the notice. Dismissing the notice leaves all gameplay rules strict. It reappears on reload if the condition persists. Reset is also available from the existing persistent button. No automatic deletion, refund, free purchase or alternative order is introduced.

The independent review identified an existing failed-reset lock: deletion failure made Cancel → Keep unusable. The committer now restores its pre-reset write state on deletion failure without flushing anything. A previously failed purchase remains blocked until explicitly retried; a previously healthy save remains usable. Both paths have focused regression coverage, and failed reset → Cancel → Keep is browser-verified.
