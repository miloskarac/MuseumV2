Implemented 2026-11-13. All changes remain uncommitted. Validation uses synthetic saves and disposable Edge contexts; no real player storage is read or changed.

## Playable content

`walchia` is the fourth Room 1 exhibit. Its single required part is `walchia-whole`, recovered as `walchia-slab`. It uses the existing whole-slab placement interaction rather than an animal skeleton rig. Discovery, manual brushing, collection, bag transport, helper custody/delivery, site chest, museum storage, stored preparation and final placement use the existing mechanics.

Walchia shares `eryops-dig`: two previously empty cells, row 0 columns 3 and 4, hold its impression at the existing pit floor. Eryops's six discoveries, all prior coordinates/burial thresholds, two soil layers, grid, site ownership and 20-coin purchase remain unchanged. Either fossil can be recovered first. Site completion now requires both; collection never grants a stand.

The stand is at X −11, Z 4.5, footprint 2.2 × 1.8. Its player marker is outside the west side; visitors use walkable positions around that footprint. The reserved stand is replaced when bought, without adding obstacles or changing Room 1 geometry.

## Purchase, admission and completion contract

The only active stand sequence is Dimetrodon 12 → Trilobite 8 → Eryops 16 → Walchia 10. Each next stand requires a valid display of its predecessor. Walchia may be collected while locked. Explicit purchase and the existing incremental deposit interaction remain necessary. Missing material, absent ownership, duplicate placement or invalid room ownership cannot count as a valid display.

Admission is 4 → 6 → 10 → 12. Only valid Walchia display adds 2. Prior paid tickets retain their amounts; waiting tickets use the price at payment. Cash stays in the existing pending ledger until collected. Arrival cadence, cashier attendance, tour duration and 6 waiting / 6 admitted limits are unchanged.

`room1Complete` projects the saved inventory/stand/room state through the existing authoritative pure room predicate. It requires all four displays. There is no saved completion flag, bonus, free room or fifth active stand. The room-completion banner is requested only by a successful incomplete-to-complete publication. It waits for the fossil information dialog to close, then displays for seven seconds or until dismissed. Reload/restore clears it; the ongoing room-complete hint remains visible. Room 2 is future content, not an active purchase.

Frozen contracts are unchanged. Default historical parser behavior is unchanged; the extra purchase, recipe, custody site and helper validation are supplied only by the schema-26 reader. New-game creation and current app loading/reset use schema 26.

Migration from validated schema 25 preserves money, reward outcomes/claims/exclusions, receipts, deposits, historical admission evidence, pending cash, existing fossils, upgrades, helper state, site ownership and west-wing credit. The only content-state adjustment removes `eryops-dig` from `closedSites`, making its new material accessible. Previously cleared soil remains cleared; it is not paid again. No Walchia material or stand is granted. Migration runs once, and a subsequently completed schema-26 site stays closed.

All earlier schemas continue through their original readers/migrations before this final step. Invalid stored data is retained for the existing recovery/reset UI. Obsolete but structurally valid development saves still enforce the current stand order and retain the manual recovery/reset flow; there are no grandfathered order exceptions. Item-changing and purchase/display operations publish only after durable save success.
