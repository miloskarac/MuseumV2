# Phase 7.7 hotfix — cash pickup and Walchia

Implemented 2026-11-16. No Git commits. The pre-existing uncommitted Phase 7 work
was preserved. No Phase 7.8, map/travel implementation, new purchase, price change,
save migration, dependency or player-save reset is included.

## Behavior

### Cash

- Cashier revenue now collects when the player's movement reaches or crosses a
  **1.1 world-unit radius** around the existing cash tray. The check includes the
  travelled segment and stationary proximity. The radius reaches the walkable
  edge of the tray while staying separate from the cashier service position.
- The manual `Покупи` button and Babylon tap callback are removed. A noninteractive
  `Приђи новчаницама` hint and green ground ring identify the pickup area.
- The existing green rectangular note meshes are clearer, with a paper stripe.
  The former round wallet icon is now a stack of banknotes. Pending cash still
  grows the world pile; collected cash grows the wallet stack and briefly shows
  `Новчанице су у торби!` with a small lift/pulse (respecting reduced motion).
  Cash pickup no longer shows `+amount`. Soil reward feedback is unchanged.
- The **exact spendable balance remains visible**. The wallet stack is a bounded
  visual summary: `ceil(sqrt(balance))`, capped at 12 notes. The pending world
  pile retains its existing 20-note cap. These are presentation limits, not
  currency limits or denominations. Spending/reloading re-derives the wallet
  stack from the current balance; there is no separate saved visual counter.
- Proximity calls the existing `collectTicketRevenue` command. It transfers the
  entire pending amount and zeros it in one candidate. The existing critical
  commit publishes state and feedback only after durable success. Repeated
  collisions see zero pending revenue; failed writes block further mutation and
  retry the same candidate once. A later ticket sale creates a new pending pile.
- Museum, active-session, focus/visibility, activity and failed-save gates remain
  in effect. Schema 26, pending-cash accounting, receipt/sales history, prices,
  rewards, purchases, loading and progression are unchanged.

### Walchia

- Root cause: CSS centered only `[data-target="trilobite-whole"]`. Walchia's
  absolutely positioned target had no coordinates and was clipped at the lower
  left of the preview.
- The shared whole-fossil mode now centers its target over the preview and uses a
  **64×64 px** indicator, clear outline/selection halo and stronger ghost art.
  This also keeps the trilobite on the same layout path. Skeleton projection
  anchors are unchanged. Click, keyboard activation and Pointer Events placement
  still use the original validation; dragging keeps its 28 px forgiving margin.
- The completed card pairs the recovered branch graphic with
  `walchia-reconstruction.svg`, original locally bundled project artwork.
  Captions distinguish `Откривени фосил · гранчица` from
  `Жива биљка · реконструкција`. The visible note states that colors and crown
  shape are conjectural. No third-party image was copied or substituted.
- The reconstruction interprets the tiered lateral branching and narrow leaves
  described in [DiMichele et al. (2007), section 4.1](https://repository.si.edu/bitstream/handle/10088/7135/paleo_2007_DiMichele_Abo_RPP_small.pdf).
  The [Sam Noble Museum gallery](https://samnoblemuseum.ou.edu/common-fossils-of-oklahoma/gallery/permian-fossil-gallery/permian-walchia-gallery/)
  supports the fossil comparison. This is a general Walchia-type interpretation,
  not a precise reconstruction of an identified species. See the asset README
  for provenance. Source links are optional reading, not runtime image requests.
- Material entitlement, placement validation, partial progress, final display,
  admission of 12 and derived Room 1 completion remain unchanged.

## Independent review

A separate read-only museum reviewer compared the hotfix with the starting-file
snapshots, traced proximity/atomic commit/failure/pause behavior, placement and
completion paths, checked artwork provenance/morphology, and inspected desktop
and phone before/after screenshots. They independently ran **41/41 tests** across
the new cash suite, Phase 6 cash regression and Walchia suites.

The reviewer initially flagged stale `#collect-cash` selectors in four Phase 7
scripts. Those were replaced with walking routes; the historical MVP harness is
explicitly identified above. Final review found **no confirmed remaining source
defects**. The reviewer did not independently run the browser harness or build;
those results are from the implementing agent. Remaining limitations are human
play feel/child comprehension, small phone overview objects, stylized rather than
species-exact artwork, and the pre-existing Vite chunk-size warning.
