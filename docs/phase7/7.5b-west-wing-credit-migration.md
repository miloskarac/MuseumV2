# Phase 7.5B — West-wing investment credit migration

## Implemented contract

The application now creates, loads and resets **schema 24** saves. Schemas 1–23 retain their historical meaning and validators. No room purchase or credit-spending command exists; no new UI, room gate, price, content, geometry or camera behavior is introduced.

Schema 24 adds this strictly validated envelope (example: seven coins invested):

```json
{
  "expansionCredit": {
    "version": 1,
    "sources": [
      { "id": "west-wing", "recognized": 7, "remaining": 7, "consumed": 0 }
    ]
  }
}
```

There is exactly one source record, including when investment is zero. `recognized` records cumulative eligible investment, `remaining` reserves that amount for a future expansion, and `consumed` records usage (necessarily zero in this version). Remaining credit cannot currently be spent. Each amount must be a safe integer between 0 and the frozen source maximum of 18. Unknown fields, sources and versions, missing/duplicate source records, nonzero consumption and malformed states are rejected.

Both recognized and remaining credit must **equal** the verified investment. Accepting a smaller value would permit stale/lost credit; accepting a larger value would fabricate credit. The frozen rules live in `src/persistence/contracts/expansion-credit-v1.ts`, independently of future live room content or prices.

## Eligible investment and migration

`parseCreditSave` first obtains a valid schema-23 base through the unchanged historical reader chain. Only then does it derive or validate the credit source:

| Valid evidence | Credit | Original evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid west-wing purchase receipt | 18 | Receipt and ownership retained, paid amount unchanged. |
| Partial west-wing payment | Exact partial amount (1–17) | Deposit retained; wing stays unowned and payable. |
| Legacy west-wing receipt with zero paid | 0 | Legacy access retained; no historical payment invented. |
| No west-wing investment | 0 | No ownership granted. |

A completed receipt replaces the partial deposit as evidence. They are never summed. The historical validators already reject simultaneous ownership and a partial deposit, duplicate/invalid receipts and invalid payment prerequisites.

For old saves, `migrateCreditSave` adds the source envelope and changes only the top-level schema version. For schema 24, the reader strips the envelope, validates the remainder as schema 23, and then checks exact agreement with the source investment. It rejects unsupported consumption rather than silently repairing it. Readers do not write or erase storage. The application persists the migrated state through its existing supported load/commit lifecycle.

Spendable coins, pending ticket revenue, ticket ledger, soil rewards, fossil progress, room ownership, museum/site ownership, helpers, custody, upgrades and all partial payments retain their established meaning. Loading still performs the existing paid-visitor retirement, helper recovery and delivery settlement; no offline income or work is added.

## Continued payments and atomicity

Both accepted payment paths call `syncExpansionCredit` before returning their candidate:

- `contributePurchase`: one coin increases the existing deposit and replaces recognized/remaining credit with the new cumulative investment.
- `buyPurchase`: either a full buy or the final one-coin contribution replaces the partial deposit with the existing 18-coin receipt and sets total credit to 18.

For example, 7 → 10 recognizes 10 in total, not 17. Continuing 10 → 18 produces one 18-coin receipt, removes the deposit and leaves exactly 18 credit. Synchronization assigns the cumulative amount, never repeatedly adds the full source investment. Other purchases retain the same credit envelope when west-wing investment is unchanged.

The application already sends these complete candidates through a critical commit. Payment and credit share one serialized save and one storage write. Failed writes leave both durable values unchanged and freeze further mutation; retry writes the frozen candidate and publishes it once without rerunning the payment command. Reload before a successful write recovers the old payment and old credit together. Reset discards pending candidates and creates zero credit. The save writer, storage key, session lock and committer are unchanged.

Repeated completed-purchase commands are no-ops. Repeated synchronization, migration/reload and retry cannot increase credit above cumulative investment. Separate accepted one-coin contributions remain separate payments by design; no new payment identifier or timing rule was introduced.

## Financial invariants and future consumption

Credit is a claim on money already spent or deposited. It is not new wealth: it is excluded from coins, pending revenue, ticket income, soil rewards and the historical financial reconciliation. The original west-wing receipt/deposit already accounts for that expenditure, so adding credit to accounting would count it twice.

**Phase 7.5C requirements, not implemented here:** approve room prices, eligible credit targets, contribution behavior and purchase prerequisites. Introduce a versioned save extension; preserve schema 24's zero-consumption meaning. Future validation must enforce `recognized = remaining + consumed`, reconcile consumed credit against actual room transactions and prevent use at multiple targets. Retain source provenance and original historical spending.

A recommended future receipt should distinguish newly paid coins from applied credit. Only newly paid coins represent additional cash expenditure; applied credit must not be counted again as fresh cash spending or refunded into the historical balance. Final accounting depends on the approved room transaction model. Do not reuse the version-1 synchronization function for consumption: its valid inputs have consumed zero and it assigns all recognized credit to remaining credit.

## Independent engineering assessment

- **Verified current scope:** the cumulative model preserves all valid source evidence, gives legacy ownership zero credit and bounds credit to actual investment. Full-snapshot migration tests preserve money/progress. Candidate-level and actual browser failure/retry checks keep the payment-credit relationship atomic. No Critical or High current-scope defect identified.
- **High priority before Phase 7.5C:** consumption needs independently validated room receipts, one-time source usage, mixed credit/cash accounting and transaction failure tests. Never enable spending by merely relaxing the consumed-zero check or changing a live price.
- **Medium maintenance risk:** runtime constructors and transition functions trust already validated application state, as before; the committer is not a second validator. Future source-changing commands must synchronize within their candidate, and new state-reconstruction paths must retain the credit field. The present assertions cover existing paths, not hypothetical future commands.
- **Low limitation:** the new amounts are intentionally redundant for explicit future accounting, so exact consistency checks are essential. Automated regressions do not prove every possible real-world save or child-facing playability. No presentation change was made.

There is no remaining credit-spending work within Phase 7.5B. Room purchases, room price decisions, consumed-credit migrations and full future excavation-graph reachability remain unimplemented/unverified by design.
