# Phase 7.7C — Visitor capacity and admission

Implemented and validated on 2026-11-12. Schema remains **25**. All changes are uncommitted; no real player storage was accessed.

## Lifecycle and occupancy

The existing simulator and saved visit records remain authoritative. No second visitor system or reservation counter was introduced.

| Stage | Representation | Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arriving / waiting for ticket | Saved visit with `paid: 0`; transient actor moves to its queue position | Waiting only |
| Ticket payment | Attended, ready front visitor completes the existing one-second service and durable payment | Changes one waiting record to paid |
| Walking / viewing | Saved positive `paid` amount; transient actor follows its existing exhibit stops | Admitted |
| Leaving | Same paid record; final transient destination is the visitor door | Admitted until physical departure |
| Exited | Simulator reaches the door, then commits `finishVisit`; actor and meshes are removed on synchronization | No reservation |

Occupancy is derived from positive paid records, including the entire exit route. Repeated payment or departure calls cannot charge or release twice. A failed departure write retains its reservation until retry commits; a failed payment publishes neither admission nor income. Scene layout changes retain actor identity and paid status, and existing route recovery continues to move actors out of newly obstructed positions.

## Admission and spawning

Payment requires the existing front-of-queue, physically ready, attended cashier checks, plus an available admitted place. The positive paid amount is written in the same candidate as its ticket-ledger sale and pending revenue. Successful persistence publishes the reservation and money together.

At full occupancy, the waiting visitor remains unpaid in the queue. Service time is not banked while full: after a departure, service takes the normal duration. The UI explains: **Музеј је пун. Чекамо да неко изађе.** It displays independent waiting and admitted counts.

Arrivals depend on waiting capacity, not admitted occupancy. Room 1 can contain six paid visits plus six waiting visits. A full waiting queue resets the arrival timer, so freeing a waiting place cannot trigger accumulated spawns. Existing 12/10/8-second arrival intervals, one-second service, movement speed, five-second base viewing time, and two-stop tours remain unchanged. No valid display means no arrivals or paid admission.

The ticket price remains 4/6/10 for the active progression. Waiting visitors pay the price at service; existing paid amounts remain locked. Cash stays pending until the existing manual collection action. No refunds or additional financial mechanics were added.

## Persistence

No saved field or schema version was added for derived capacity. Existing schema-25 payloads remain readable. Its reader now validates waiting and admitted counts independently, deriving the limits from validated room ownership. It rejects seven waiting or seven admitted records even when the ledger and money are otherwise balanced.

The shared ledger reader receives an explicit current-reader limit policy. Its historical default remains six total visits and three waiting visits; schema-24 and earlier readers/contracts are unchanged. The historical Economy v2 research simulator now reads those old limits from the frozen contract, preserving its reproducible baseline after the live capacity change.

Position, viewing timer and exit path are still transient. **Reload and travel retain the established behavior:** retire paid tours without changing committed sales or pending cash, retain unpaid queue records, and rebuild actors safely. They are deliberate session-replacement boundaries, not ordinary in-scene exits. During uninterrupted museum simulation, reservations release only at physical departure and successful commit. Reset uses the existing separately confirmed new-game flow and restores Room 1 capacity; it introduces no extra charge or silent revenue loss.
